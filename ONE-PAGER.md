**ComplianceForge**
Governed compliance evidence for industrial training

**The problem**

When an HSE inspector asks for proof that training was compliant, most organisations produce a PowerPoint and a sign-in sheet. That is not evidence. There is no record of what the content said, who reviewed it, what was changed, or whether the person who signed it off was qualified to do so. UK employers face 14,000 proactive HSE inspections in 2025/26, and the standard of proof expected — particularly around MHSWR 1999 Regulation 7 (competent person involvement) — is rising. AI-generated training content makes this worse, not better, unless the review and governance layer is built in from the start.

**What ComplianceForge does**

ComplianceForge generates bespoke compliance training from a workplace description and regulatory classification, then enforces a structured human review workflow before anything reaches a learner. The output is not training content alone — it is a complete, locked evidence chain proving that content was generated against specific regulations, reviewed by a named competent person, attested with recorded rationale, and delivered with certificate linkage and an immutable audit trail.

**What is built and working today**

The platform is live in demo with a seeded pilot tenant (warehousing / roller racking, five regulatory schemas, three review findings, full attestation, certificate chain, and audit pack export). Shipped capabilities:

- Multi-agent generation pipeline — workplace-specific content backed by UK regulatory source documents, not templates
- Reviewer portal — structured findings review with severity classification, mandatory disposition, and non-bypassable attestation workflow
- Certificate chain — module certificates and learner certificates linked by SHA-256 content hashes, traceable to source legislation
- WORM audit log — database-level immutability enforced by Postgres triggers, not application logic
- Snapshot version history with content diff — proves what changed between review cycles
- Audit evidence pack — single multi-page PDF assembling the full compliance evidence chain for external recipients (inspectors, insurers, auditors)

**Why this matters commercially**

The buyer is not a training manager. The buyer is the H&S director, operations director, or risk manager who needs to demonstrate governance — to an inspector, an insurer, or a tribunal. The value is not measured against the cost of producing training. It is measured against the cost of failing an inspection, losing an insurance dispute, or defending an incident without evidence that training was properly governed.

Pricing: GBP 1,000–1,500 per module. Cost of goods: approximately GBP 1 in API costs per generation.

**Ideal customers**

- Industrial operators with regulatory inspection exposure (warehousing, manufacturing, construction, energy, logistics)
- Multi-site organisations needing consistent, evidenced training across locations
- H&S consultancies advising clients on defensible training governance
- Insurers seeking structured competency evidence from policyholders

**Why now**

HSE enforcement is intensifying. The HSE/ICO 2026 joint guidance requires "meaningful human involvement" in AI-assisted compliance tools. Most AI training products have no review workflow, no attestation, no audit trail, and no certificate linkage. The regulatory environment is moving toward exactly the evidence standard ComplianceForge already provides.

**What we are looking for**

- Pilot customers in regulated industries willing to run a live module through the full workflow
- Strategic partners in H&S consultancy, industrial insurance, or compliance software
- Early commercial backing to support go-to-market and the first paid deployments
