# Enterprise Positioning Brief (External) — Vestium

## 1. Executive Positioning

Vestium helps regulated UK operators produce inspection-ready compliance evidence with accountable human review, while reducing the time needed to create modules, SOPs, and RAMS.

It combines three workflows in one platform:
- **Create**: generate role- and workplace-specific modules, SOPs, and RAMS
- **Learn**: deliver competency-focused learning and assessment
- **Audit**: maintain review, sign-off, and verification records for assurance teams

## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary ICP
- UK operators in warehousing, logistics, manufacturing, construction, utilities, and facilities management
- Typical size: 50–500 employees
- Operating under HSE-enforced regimes (for example MHSWR, PUWER, LOLER, COSHH, CDM)
- Need to demonstrate workforce competence and accountable oversight

### Buyer Group
- **Primary buyer**: Head of H&S / Compliance Director
- **Economic buyer**: Operations Director or Finance sponsor
- **Influencers**: HR/L&D, site managers, auditors, insurer-facing risk leads

### Typical Buying Triggers
- Upcoming inspection or external audit
- Insurance renewal requiring stronger evidence
- Process/site change needing rapid rollout of new controls
- Incident follow-up requiring documented corrective learning

## 3. Core Problems We Solve

1. **Weak defensibility of compliance evidence**
- Many teams can show completion, but cannot clearly show who reviewed content, what changed, and why it was approved.

2. **Slow, manual content lifecycle**
- Drafting, reviewing, and updating modules/SOPs/RAMS is often fragmented and time-heavy.

3. **Inconsistent governance across sites**
- Multi-site operators struggle to maintain consistent standards and traceable approvals.

4. **Costly change management after updates**
- Regulatory/process changes force urgent content updates with limited internal capacity.

## 4. Product Value Proposition

Vestium provides a governed lifecycle:

**Draft -> Review -> Attest -> Deploy -> Verify**

Key value:
- Named reviewer accountability at release point
- Verifiable evidence trail linking issued outcomes to approved content versions
- Better readiness for insurer, client, and regulator scrutiny
- Faster content production as a secondary operational benefit

## 5. Differentiation (External-Safe)

Compared with generic LMS or standalone AI authoring tools, Vestium is differentiated by:
- Built-in governance workflow, not optional approval steps
- Human-review-first controls for high-risk findings
- Strong traceability from approved content to issued certificates/evidence
- Unified handling of modules, SOPs, and RAMS in the same governance model

To our knowledge, few platforms combine AI-assisted drafting, accountable reviewer sign-off, and evidence-oriented verification in one operating model for UK H&S workflows.

## 6. Objection Handling

### “We already have an LMS.”
That is common. LMS platforms typically deliver learning; Vestium governs creation, review, and evidence. It is designed to complement existing LMS infrastructure.

### “AI content won’t stand up to scrutiny.”
AI output alone is not enough. Vestium is designed around human review and accountable sign-off before deployment.

### “How do we handle regulation or process changes?”
The platform supports controlled regeneration, re-review, and re-deployment workflows, so updates are managed as governed changes rather than ad-hoc edits.

### “We need SOPs and RAMS as well.”
Modules, SOPs, and RAMS are supported in the same workflow model, reducing tool sprawl and governance inconsistency.

## 7. Commercial Model (Illustrative)

Pricing depends on deployment scope, usage, and governance requirements. Typical commercial structures:
- Per-module / per-artifact services for initial rollout
- Subscription for ongoing Create/Learn/Audit operations
- Add-ons for advanced reporting, audit-defense packs, and enterprise controls

Commercial value is usually framed against:
- reduced authoring/review overhead
- faster deployment cycles
- lower audit-preparation effort
- improved insurer/client confidence

All ranges should be validated during discovery and proposal scoping.

## 8. Proof Approach for Enterprise Buyers

Use measurable before/after outcomes during pilot:
- draft-to-release cycle time
- review rounds per artifact
- evidence prep hours per audit cycle
- percentage of outputs with complete reviewer traceability

Recommended pilot duration: 30–90 days, with one high-friction workflow first, then scale to additional teams/sites.
